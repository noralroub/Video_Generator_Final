Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

What Infodemica Processes

Infodemica turns public biomedical papers into narrated visual summaries. To do that, the app stores your account details, the paper IDs you submit, generation status, and generated files such as scripts, audio, HTML presentations, and logs.

AI Providers

Submitted paper content and generated prompts may be sent to configured AI providers, including Anthropic and Google Gemini, so the app can create narration, visuals, and audio.

Your Controls

  • You can delete generated outputs from your account on the status, result, or My Videos pages.
  • You can reset your password from the login page if your account has an email address.
  • Do not submit private patient information or unpublished confidential material.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Infodemica administrator for this deployment.
